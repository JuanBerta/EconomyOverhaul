# Mod Description:
This mod for Elin adds an economic layer to the game: a bank with daily interest,
an investable market (stocks and real estate) with daily income/expenses, and a
loan system to borrow gold when you need it. The idea of this mod is to give the
player non-game-map-visible ways of making, saving, and borrowing the in-game
currency.

## Features (v0.26.0)

1- Bank account: deposit and withdraw gold, earn daily compound interest
2- Investment market: buy and sell stocks and real estate through an in-game UI
3- Daily settlement: investments generate income and expenses every day
4- Loan system: borrow gold from the bank and repay it partially or in full
5- Full English / Spanish localization (config `UI.Language`)
6- Saves integrate with the game save (Steam Cloud friendly), versioned and
   robust against corruption

## Roadmap

- Future Markets for all in-game items that are tradeable
- Owning businesses that generate income and expenses, with staff and
  management (automation via hired business managers)
- Hiring an accountant to reduce taxes and make autonomous investments
  (with a minimum amount that can be set as untouchable)
- Buying in-game cities and founding new ones with selectable specialization
- City economies with production and consumption of goods
- Trading caravans owned by the player and NPCs, trading goods between cities
- Natural migration system based on job opportunities, happiness, GDP, etc.
